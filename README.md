## About

This repository contains the **showcase website for my research thesis on CC-LoRA**.

The website presents the research journey, methodology, experiments, results, and key findings of the thesis in an interactive and visual format.

Rather than serving as the research codebase, this repository is intended as a **digital presentation of the research work**.

---

## Research

The thesis investigates **CC-LoRA** in the context of **parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT)** and explores how pretrained models can be adapted while reducing the computational and parameter requirements associated with conventional fine-tuning.

The research covers the motivation behind efficient model adaptation, the proposed approach, experimental evaluation, and analysis of the obtained results.

---

## What's Inside

The website brings together the main components of the thesis:

* **Research Motivation** — The problem and motivation behind the work
* **Background** — Relevant concepts and prior approaches
* **Methodology** — The CC-LoRA approach and experimental framework
* **Experiments** — Experimental setup and evaluation
